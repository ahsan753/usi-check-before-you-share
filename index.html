How it works

Six posts in Omar's class chat. Would you share them?

Some are real. Some are fake. You cannot tell by looking — so you check.

SStop — how does it make you feel?
IInvestigate — who posted it?
FFind — is it anywhere else?
TTrace — where is the original?

Use at least two checks on each post. Then the Share it and Don't share it buttons unlock.

Pairs: one person clicks, one person reads. The reader decides — and says which check settled it.
